 My project brief

## The question
Which wards in Osogbo LGA are located more than 5 km from a designated public market?

## The data I need
- Ward boundaries — GRID3 — https://data.grid3.org — GeoPackage — 12 MB
- Settlement extents — GRID3 — https://data.grid3.org — GeoPackage — 40 MB
- Watercourses — OSM via QuickOSM — extracted for my LGA
- Elevation — Copernicus DEM 30 m — https://portal.opentopography.org
